August 1, 2024 Coordinator | Statements and Commentary

The Head of Global Citizens Warmly Welcomes the Historic Exchange of Detainees Between Russia and the West

The Head of the Global Citizens International Movement warmly welcomes the exchange of detainees that took place between Russia and Western countries.

The outcome achieved is of important humanitarian significance and confirms the continuing relevance of direct dialogue and political and diplomatic means in addressing complex issues. We express our appreciation to all participants in the negotiating process who contributed to the preparation and implementation of the exchange.
